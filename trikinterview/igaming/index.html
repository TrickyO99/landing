iGaming jobs FR.

A French-language job board that gathers the openings of the online gambling operators licensed by the ANJ, the French regulator, and of the big iGaming suppliers, so there is no need to visit each careers site by hand.

Open the board 1,400+ offers, updated daily

How it works

  • Sources. 14 companies are scraped today. Operators: bwin/partypoker (Entain), PokerStars (Flutter), bet365, Betsson, Betclic, FDJ United, Tipico and LeoVegas. iGaming suppliers: Evolution, Playtech, Sportradar, Genius Sports, Kaizen Gaming (Betano) and Playson. All of them publish open job feeds.
  • What is missing, and why. Winamax and PMU list their jobs on Welcome to the Jungle, which answers automated visitors with a 403, and the board does not try to get around that. They show as "not monitored", like Circus, NetBet, Vbet, Genybet, Olybet and Feelingbet, whose scrapers aren't written yet.
  • How it reads them. One small adapter per source, on public job feeds (SmartRecruiters, Greenhouse, Ashby, Workday) or a sitemap when a site has no feed. robots.txt is respected. Requests are rate-limited and retried politely, and the scraper runs once a day.
  • What it stores. Title, location and country, contract type, description and a category worked out from the title (Support, Tech / Data, Marketing, Legal, Finance and so on). Each run marks offers that disappeared as inactive instead of deleting them.
  • Right now. 1,418 active offers, mostly in tech, data and customer support, many of them abroad. Filter by country to narrow it down.
  • The site. Next.js with a local SQLite file. Filterable list, one page per offer with JobPosting structured data, sitemap and robots.txt. It runs on my home server behind a Cloudflare tunnel, with the scraper as a second container on the same database.
